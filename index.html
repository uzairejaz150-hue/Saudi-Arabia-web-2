13 Provinces of Saudi Arabia

Riyadh

Capital of Saudi Arabia and a major business and cultural center.

Makkah

Home to the Holy Kaaba and Masjid al-Haram.

Madinah

A historic Islamic city and home to Al Masjid an Nabawi.

Eastern Province

Known for its coastline, modern cities and energy industry.

Asir

Famous for mountains, green landscapes and beautiful scenery.

Tabuk

A northern region known for mountains and historical sites.

Hail

Known for its desert landscapes and ancient rock art.

Al-Qassim

Famous for agriculture, dates and traditional culture.

Jazan

A coastal region known for beaches and natural beauty.

Najran

Known for its ancient heritage and traditional architecture.

Al-Baha

A beautiful mountainous region with forests and valleys.

Al-Jouf

Known for historical sites, agriculture and olive farms.

Northern Borders

A northern region with desert landscapes and heritage sites.
